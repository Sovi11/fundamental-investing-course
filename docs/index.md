# Fundamental Investing — From Zero

**An in-depth, India-first course in fundamental equity analysis** — from "what is a balance sheet?" to building a
three-statement model, valuing banks, catching accounting fraud, writing an investment memo and sizing a position.

It is written for numerate people who know markets but have never studied accounting: traders, engineers,
quants, analysts from other fields. It assumes nothing about finance theory and goes deep quickly.

!!! warning "Educational material, not investment advice"
    Nothing in this course is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Real companies appear as historical
    illustrations of method. Two running-example companies (Kaveri Pumps and Nirmal Finance) are entirely fictional.
    See [Disclaimer](disclaimer.md).

## How the course works

- **Lessons** follow one template: why it matters → objectives → concepts with worked examples → *Trader's lens* and
  *India notes* callouts → common mistakes → key terms → self-check questions with hidden answers → further reading.
- **Two fictional running-example companies** carry through every module so that the same numbers reappear in
  accounting, ratios, valuation, forensics, modelling and the mocks:
  [Kaveri Pumps & Motors](appendix/running-example/kaveri-pumps.md) (a manufacturer) and
  [Nirmal Finance](appendix/running-example/nirmal-finance.md) (an NBFC). Their statements are generated by code and
  always balance. Kaveri also has a [reference valuation](appendix/running-example/kaveri-valuation.md).
- **Every module ends with exercises** in four tiers — Warm-up, Core, Stretch and a *Real-world task* on an actual
  listed Indian company — with fully worked solutions.
- **Mocks** at four checkpoints plus a four-hour final exam and a stock-pitch mock.
- **Case studies** are written "you are the analyst": you get the information available at the time, answer questions,
  then read what happened and what was knowable in advance.
