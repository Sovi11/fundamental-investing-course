# Module 08 · Sector Playbooks (India)

**What this module does.** Eleven field guides, one per sector, each in the same shape: how the industry makes
money → value chain and structure → KPIs and where to find them → what drives earnings and multiples → accounting
quirks → red flags → how to value → representative listed companies (for study, not recommendation) → a
ten-question checklist. Dated to September 2026; every real-company fact must be re-verified before use.

**Why it matters.** The generic toolkit of Modules 04–07 is necessary; the sector's own KPIs and regulatory
levers are what an analyst is actually judged on. A bank analyst who does not know the ECL transition date, or an
auto analyst who does not read VAHAN, is not doing the job.

## How to use the playbooks

- Read the sector you are researching *before* the company; run the ten-question checklist on every company in
  it.
- Each playbook's "representative companies" are the study set for building your own 10-year dashboards
  ([04.6](../04-financial-analysis/06-per-share-metrics-and-ratio-dashboard.md)) — the fastest route to a circle
  of competence ([11.1](../11-process/01-idea-generation.md)).
- Dates matter: the regulatory and cycle facts here are a snapshot; the checklists are durable.
