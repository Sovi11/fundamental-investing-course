# 08.10 · Telecom, media & internet platforms

> **Sector playbook.** Telecom is a three-player market whose profits now depend on tariff discipline (a ~15%
> hike was the 2026 base case) and whose weakest player survives on government forbearance; media is being
> reshaped by streaming and the JioStar combination; internet platforms are the 2021 IPO cohort growing into
> their valuations — or not. Metrics: ARPU, subscribers, capex intensity; GMV, take rate, contribution margin.
> As of September 2026.

**Time:** ~70 min · **Prerequisites:** [07.4 High-growth & loss-making companies](../07-special-valuation/04-high-growth-and-loss-making.md), [07.6 §4 Telecom](../07-special-valuation/06-real-estate-infra-utilities-telecom.md)

## 1. Telecom

| Item | Notes |
|:--|:--|
| **Structure** | Reliance Jio (~40%+ subscriber share), Bharti Airtel (~35%), Vodafone Idea (~18%), BSNL (residual); Jio and Airtel take essentially all the industry's cash profit — verify current TRAI shares |
| **ARPU (Q1 FY27)** | Airtel ₹264 (highest), Jio ~₹210–215, Vi ₹177 ([TelecomTalk](https://telecomtalk.info/airtel-reliance-jio-vodafone-idea-arpu-q1fy27/1010651/)); industry expects a 10–20% tariff hike in 2026 ([Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/indian-telecom-tariff-hike-2026-prices-cmo9oadyzug1hph0jbgloei2r)) |
| **KPIs** | ARPU; subscribers (total, active/VLR, 4G/5G, postpaid mix); churn; data usage per user (Jio ~44 GB/month); EBITDA margin (Airtel India mobile > 55%); capex intensity (5G build tapering from 2025); net debt incl. spectrum and AGR; Africa (Airtel), home broadband and enterprise (both), FWA (Jio AirFiber) |
| **Drivers** | Tariff hikes (the only lever that moves industry revenue — three since 2019); subscriber migration from Vi to the leaders; 5G monetisation (weak so far); spectrum auctions and renewals; AGR/regulatory dues; government policy toward Vi (equity conversions 2023 and 2025 raised the government's stake — verify current %; bank guarantee waivers; AGR relief debates) |
| **Valuation** | EV/EBITDA (8–12x for the leaders, verify) with spectrum liabilities in EV; DCF with capex fade; SOTP for Airtel (India mobile, Africa, Indus Towers stake, broadband); Vi on the Merton option lens ([06.7](../06-valuation/07-other-valuation-methods.md); [case I10](../13-case-studies/india/10-vodafone-idea-telecom-war.md)) |
| **Red flags** | Subscriber counts inflated by inactive SIMs (use VLR); capex "guidance" that keeps slipping; AGR/dues accounting (provision vs contingent); related-party payments within the Reliance group (Jio Platforms is not separately listed — verify) |
| **Adjacent** | Towers (Indus Towers: tenancies, Vi receivables); fibre/data centres; Tata Communications (enterprise) |

## 4. Accounting quirks

Telecom: spectrum as intangible assets with deferred liabilities; AGR provisions vs contingents; Ind AS 116 tower
leases; capitalised network costs; revenue from prepaid recharges (deferred until used). Media: content
capitalisation and amortisation policies; sports rights amortisation; NTO-driven subscription accounting.
Platforms: gross vs net revenue (Ind AS 115 principal/agent — food delivery revenue is *net* of restaurant
share; marketplace GMV is not revenue); delivery-partner costs; ESOP charges; "adjusted EBITDA" definitions;
one-off gains on stake sales (Info Edge, Eternal's investments).

## 5. Red flags

Telecom: ARPU growth only from tariff hikes while subscribers leak; capex deferrals; Vi's receivables at towers
and vendors. Media: ad growth lagging viewership; content write-offs; promoter pledges. Platforms: GMV growth via
discounts booked as marketing (or netted from revenue), take rates falling, contribution-margin definitions
changing quarter to quarter, cohort disclosure dropped, ESOP grants accelerating before a price rise, related-party
dealings with founders' other ventures.

## 6. The 10-question sector checklist

1. Telecom: ARPU trajectory, tariff-hike timing, and the split of ARPU growth between price and mix?
2. Subscriber quality: VLR/active share, churn, 5G/4G mix?
3. Capex intensity and free cash flow after spectrum payments?
4. Total liabilities including spectrum and AGR; government stake and forbearance risk (Vi)?
5. Media: ad-cycle position; subscription regulation; content-cost trend vs revenue?
6. Platforms: contribution margin per unit and its trend — rebuilt from the P&L?
7. Cumulative burn, cash runway, and the dilution path (ESOPs, rounds)?
8. Take-rate and regulatory risk (commissions caps, payments regulation, gaming law)?
9. Competition: who is subsidising, with what balance sheet, and for how long?
10. What terminal margin and growth does EV/Sales or EV/EBITDA imply, and has anyone achieved it?
