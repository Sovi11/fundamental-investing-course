# Module 03 · Reading Filings & Documents

**What this module does.** Turns the accounting of Module 02 into the practical skill of opening any real Indian
document — annual report, quarterly results, concall transcript, rating rationale, DRHP, broker report, US
10-K — and knowing where to look, what it means and what it hides. It ends with annotated walkthroughs of
fictional excerpts seeded with green and red flags.

**Why it matters.** Analysts are paid to read what others skim. The notes to accounts, the auditor's report and
the concall transcript contain most of what decides an investment; the chairman's letter contains almost none of
it.

## You're ready to move on when you can…

- [ ] For any question about a listed Indian company, name the document that answers it and where to find it.
- [ ] Read an auditor's report and state the opinion type, the KAMs, any EoM, and the CARO items that matter.
- [ ] Open the notes in the right order and extract: RPTs, contingent liabilities, borrowings and covenants,
      receivables ageing, revenue policy, tax reconciliation.
- [ ] Read a quarterly filing and a concall transcript, normalise one-offs, and add a row to a say-do table.
- [ ] Summarise a rating rationale's liquidity position and key sensitivities in five lines.
- [ ] Annotate a fictional excerpt and match at least 80% of the model annotations.
