## Core

**E07.07** *Nirmal RoA tree.* Using FY26 figures (interest income 1,073.2; interest expense 442.1; fees 50.8; opex
241.3; credit cost 120.6; tax 80.5; average assets 6,831.4; average equity 1,590.55), build the tree in % of average
assets and derive RoE.

**E07.08** *Asset-quality roll-forward.* Stage 3 opened FY26 at ₹150.3 Cr and closed at ₹200.7 Cr; write-offs were
₹84.6 Cr. Estimate gross slippages (ignore recoveries) and express them as % of average loans (6,350).

**E07.09** *Capital.* Nirmal's CRAR is 27.6% with RWA ₹6,642 Cr. If loans grow 20% a year and RWA density holds,
with PAT growing 15% and a 12.5% payout, estimate CRAR after three years (Tier-2 constant at ₹140 Cr). When would a
15% floor bind?

**E07.10** *Justified P/B.* Compute Nirmal's justified P/B at RoE 15.1%, $k_e$ 13%, g 6%, 7% and 8%; and the implied
RoE at ₹402 (book ₹154.1/share) with g 8%.

**E07.11** *Residual income.* Value Nirmal with RoE 15.5%, 15.5%, 15%, 15%, 15% for five years (book growing 13%,
13%, 12%, 12%, 11%), then RoE 14% and g 6% for ever, $k_e$ 13%; report value per share (11 Cr shares) and P/B.

**E07.12** *Stress.* Recompute Nirmal's FY26 PAT and RoE at a credit cost of 4.5% of average loans and a cost of
funds 100 bps higher (average borrowings ≈ 5,082).

**E07.13** *Life insurer.* EV ₹15,000 Cr; VNB ₹1,050 Cr; $k_e$ 12%; VNB growth 15% for five years then 5% terminal.
Compute the structural value, appraisal value and justified P/EV.

**E07.14** *AMC.* AUM ₹2,50,000 Cr, 60% equity at 55 bp net yield, 40% debt/liquid at 10 bp; costs ₹550 Cr. Compute
operating profit and its change if the equity yield is cut 8 bp.

**E07.15** *Exchange stress.* An exchange earns ₹3,000 Cr from index options, ₹800 Cr from cash equities and ₹700 Cr
from data/listing/other, with fixed costs ₹1,200 Cr. Compute EBIT; then EBIT if index-option revenue falls 40%
after a regulatory change.

**E07.16** *Cyclical normalisation.* Narmada Steel: 6.5 MT volumes; ten-year EBITDA/tonne of 3,200, 4,500, 6,800,
9,500, 12,500, 15,000, 7,000, 4,200, 5,500, 8,000 ₹/t; D&A ₹1,800 Cr; interest ₹900 Cr; tax 25%; market cap ₹30,000
Cr; net debt ₹12,000 Cr; capacity 7 MT. Compute normalised EBITDA (mean and median), normalised PAT and P/E,
EV/tonne, and the EBITDA sensitivity to ₹1,000/t with 15% cost linkage.

**E07.17** *Cost curve.* Producers' cash costs: A ₹30k/t (2 MT), B ₹34k (3 MT), C ₹38k (3 MT), D ₹44k (2 MT), E ₹48k
(2 MT). Demand is 9 MT. Which producer is marginal, what price clears the market (at marginal cash cost), and what
is A's cash margin? Repeat for demand of 11 MT.

**E07.18** *Loss-maker path.* Dhruv Delivery: base revenue ₹2,160 Cr; orders 26.67 Cr; contribution per order −₹5
→ +₹3, +₹10, +₹16, +₹22 over five years; order growth 40/32/25/20/15%; fixed costs ₹900 Cr growing 8% from year 2.
Compute EBITDA each year, the break-even year and cumulative burn to the trough.

**E07.19** *Implied terminal multiple.* Dhruv trades at 6x revenue (EV ₹12,960 Cr). If year-8 revenue is ₹9,165 Cr
at a 12% margin and the discount rate is 14%, compute the implied EV/EBITDA on year-8 EBITDA, nominal and in
present-value terms.

**E07.20** *Dilution.* A loss-maker issues 3% new shares a year for four years via ESOPs and a ₹1,000 Cr round at
₹250/share on a 20 Cr share base in year 2. Compute the cumulative dilution factor for an existing shareholder.

**E07.21** *Holdco SOTP.* Deccan Investments: stakes of 35% in A (mcap ₹18,000 Cr), 22% in B (₹9,000 Cr), 100% of an
unlisted NBFC (₹1,200 Cr); 5 Cr shares; stock ₹950. Compute gross NAV/share and the discount; then build a
discount from causes (tax 10%, dividend leakage 15%, costs 1%, control 20%, liquidity 5%, optionality −5%).

**E07.22** *MNC royalty.* A subsidiary pays 4.5% royalty on ₹5,000 Cr sales with PAT ₹600 Cr. Compute the royalty as
% of pre-royalty PBT (assume the royalty is fully deductible, tax 25%), the annual PAT leak vs a 2% "fair" royalty,
and its capitalised value at 30x.

**E07.23** *Real-estate NAV.* Projects: A sales ₹1,200 Cr, cost 65%, 3 years; B ₹800 Cr, 55%, 4 years; C ₹2,000 Cr,
40%, 5 years (cash flows at mid-point, $k_e$ 14%); land bank ₹600 Cr; rental NOI ₹150 Cr at an 8% cap rate; net debt
₹900 Cr; 20 Cr shares. Compute NAV per share; then with a 9% cap rate and C's sales −20%.

**E07.24** *Regulated utility.* Regulated equity ₹4,000 Cr at an allowed RoE of 15.5%; $k_e$ 11%; equity base growing
6% through capex. Compute regulated PAT, justified P/B and equity value; then the value if the regulator cuts the
allowed RoE to 14%.

**E07.25** *Telecom.* An operator has 300 mn subscribers, ARPU ₹210, EBITDA margin 50%, capex ₹35,000 Cr, net debt
incl. spectrum ₹1,60,000 Cr, and trades at an EV of ₹4,00,000 Cr. Compute revenue, EBITDA, EV/EBITDA, FCF before
interest, and the EBITDA change from a 15% tariff hike with 90% flow-through.
