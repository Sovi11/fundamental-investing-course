# Module 07 · Valuing Special Kinds of Companies

**What this module does.** Adapts the toolkit of Module 06 to the companies it does not fit: lenders (no FCFF;
P/B, residual income, the RoA tree — worked on Nirmal Finance), insurers, AMCs, exchanges and brokers,
cyclicals and commodities (normalised earnings, EV/tonne, cost curves), high-growth and loss-making companies
(unit economics, path to profitability, implied terminal margins), holding companies, conglomerates, PSUs and MNC
subsidiaries, and real estate, infrastructure, utilities and telecom.

**Why it matters.** Half of India's market capitalisation is in financials, cyclicals, government-linked and
regulated businesses. Using a manufacturer's DCF on them produces confident nonsense.
