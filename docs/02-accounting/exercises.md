# Module 02 · Exercises

Numbers for Kaveri Pumps are on the [running-example page](../appendix/running-example/kaveri-pumps.md); use
them exactly. Work every numerical problem by hand or in Python before opening the [solutions](solutions.md).

## Warm-up

**E02.01** State the accounting equation and explain, with one example each, how a transaction can (a) change
assets only, (b) change assets and liabilities, (c) change assets and equity.

**E02.02** Define: accrual, deferred revenue, accrued expense, prepaid expense. Which side of the balance sheet
does each sit on?

**E02.03** List the five steps of Ind AS 115 revenue recognition.

**E02.04** Which of the following are Schedule III line items and which are analyst conventions: revenue from
operations, EBITDA, other income, EBIT, exceptional items, PBT, adjusted EPS, OCI?

**E02.05** Name four things that are *not* on a company's balance sheet but affect its value.

**E02.06** What are the three sections of the cash-flow statement, and in which does each of these appear under
Kaveri's policy: interest paid on term loans; purchase of liquid mutual funds; dividends paid; lease payments;
taxes paid?

**E02.07** Define: CWIP, right-of-use asset, deferred tax liability, non-controlling interest, goodwill.

**E02.08** Why is LIFO not permitted under Ind AS, and what difference does that make when comparing an Indian
company with a US company that uses LIFO in a period of rising input prices?

## Core (Kaveri Pumps unless stated)

**E02.09** From the FY26 P&L lines (revenue 1,318.0; materials 858.0; employees 117.3; other expenses 160.8; D&A
47.8; other income 3.9; finance costs 17.0; exceptional 0; tax 30.5), compute EBITDA, EBIT, PBT, PAT, EBITDA
margin, PAT margin, basic EPS (6.00 Cr shares) and diluted EPS (6.07 Cr).

**E02.10** FY25 PAT was ₹97.4 Cr including a ₹14.0 Cr pre-tax gain on sale of land. Compute adjusted PAT and
adjusted EPS at the 25.17% tax rate, and the underlying EPS growth into FY26.

**E02.11** Roll forward other equity from FY25 (₹607.2 Cr) to FY26 using PAT ₹90.5 Cr, dividends paid ₹24.0 Cr
and share-based payment ₹2.4 Cr. Check against the balance sheet.

**E02.12** Roll forward gross block (FY25 ₹780.0 Cr; FY26 capex to PP&E ₹36.0 Cr; transfers from CWIP ₹14.0 Cr;
no disposals) and accumulated depreciation (FY25 ₹295.1 Cr; FY26 PP&E depreciation ₹39.6 Cr). Compute FY26 net
block and check against the balance sheet.

**E02.13** Roll forward the lease liability from FY25 (₹16.7 Cr) with new leases ₹6.0 Cr, lease interest ₹1.4 Cr
and lease payments ₹5.9 Cr. Explain where each item appears in the P&L and cash-flow statement.

**E02.14** Rebuild FY26 CFO from PBT ₹121.0 Cr using: D&A 47.8; finance costs 17.0; other income 3.9; SBC 2.4;
changes in inventories (188.1 vs 161.3), receivables (346.7 vs 269.7), other current assets (39.5 vs 35.2), payables
(143.4 vs 132.3), other current liabilities (65.9 vs 58.6); current tax paid 29.5.

**E02.15** Compute FY26 CFI from: capex on PP&E incl. CWIP 48.0; intangibles 4.0; change in current investments
(15.0 vs 30.0); asset sale 0; interest received 3.9. Then compute CFF from: term-loan movement (92.0 vs 112.0);
working-capital loans (96.0 vs 58.0); interest paid 15.6; lease payments 5.9; dividends 24.0. Verify that
CFO + CFI + CFF equals the change in cash (32.5 vs 28.0).

**E02.16** Restate Kaveri's FY26 CFO under the alternative Ind AS 7 policy of including interest paid *and*
received in operating activities. What is CFF under that policy?

**E02.17** Kaveri's effective tax rate: compute it for FY25 and FY26 and explain why it equals the statutory
25.17%. What would a 15% effective rate suggest?

**E02.18** The deferred tax liability rose from ₹21.0 Cr to ₹22.0 Cr in FY26. Explain what timing difference
produces a DTL for a manufacturer, and why the ₹1.0 Cr movement is a non-cash item in the CFO reconciliation.

**E02.19** *Working capital (fictional).* A company has revenue ₹500 Cr, cost of goods ₹300 Cr, inventory days 60
(on COGS), receivable days 45 (on revenue), payable days 50 (on COGS). Compute inventory, receivables, payables
and net trade working capital.

**E02.20** *Depreciation (fictional).* An asset costs ₹120 Cr with a residual value of 5% and an 8-year life.
Compute year-1 depreciation under straight-line and under 25% written-down value; and year-2 under WDV. Which
method reports higher early profit?

**E02.21** *Deferred tax (fictional).* Book profit ₹100 Cr; book depreciation ₹10 Cr; tax depreciation ₹15 Cr;
tax rate 25%. Compute current tax, total tax expense, the deferred tax charge and the direction of the DTL.

**E02.22** *Ind AS 116 (fictional).* A five-year lease has annual rent of ₹12 Cr paid in arrears; the incremental
borrowing rate is 8%. Compute the initial lease liability and ROU asset, year-1 interest and year-1 amortisation
(straight line), and show the effect on EBITDA, EBIT and PBT in year 1 compared with treating the rent as an
operating expense.

**E02.23** *Revenue recognition.* Kaveri sells a solar pumping system to a state agency for ₹5 lakh: the pump
(delivered in March), installation (completed in April), and a two-year maintenance contract. Under Ind AS 115,
how should revenue be allocated and when recognised, and which balance-sheet items arise at 31 March?

**E02.24** *Consolidation.* A parent owns 80% of a subsidiary with revenue ₹200 Cr and PAT ₹20 Cr, and 30% of an
associate with PAT ₹50 Cr. Show how each appears in the consolidated revenue, PAT and NCI lines.

**E02.25** *Nirmal Finance.* From the FY26 P&L (interest income 1,073.2; interest expense 442.1; fees 50.8; opex
241.3; credit cost 120.6; tax 80.5), compute NII, PPOP, PBT, PAT, NIM on average loans (5,780 → 6,920), cost-to-income,
credit cost as % of average loans, RoA on average assets (6,229.1 → 7,433.7) and RoE on average equity (1,485.8 →
1,695.3).

**E02.26** Kaveri's FY26 quarterly PATs are 26.7, 12.7, 17.1 and 34.0. Show that they sum to the annual figure and
explain why Q2 is the weakest quarter.

## Stretch

**E02.27** A company changes its revenue policy for long-term contracts from completion to percentage-of-completion.
Describe the effect on revenue, receivables/unbilled revenue and CFO in the year of change, and what disclosure
Ind AS 8 requires.

**E02.28** Two companies have identical cash flows; one capitalises ₹30 Cr of development cost a year (amortised
over 5 years), the other expenses it. Compare their EBITDA, EBIT, PAT, CFO, CFI and free cash flow in year 1 and in
steady state (year 6 onward).

**E02.29** Explain why a company's consolidated cash can be ₹500 Cr while it cannot pay a dividend, and what the
standalone statements would show.

**E02.30** Kaveri's related-party purchases were ₹89.2 Cr in FY26. Where do they appear in each statement, what
does Ind AS 24 require to be disclosed, and what does "arm's length" mean in that note?

**E02.31** Using the running-example page, list every FY26 item you would classify as a *choice* (estimate or
policy) rather than a *fact*, and estimate the PAT effect of moving each to the conservative end of its range.

## Real-world task

**E02.32** Download the latest annual report of any Nifty 500 manufacturer. Rebuild EBITDA (excluding other
income), EBIT, PBT and PAT from the face of the consolidated P&L; then rebuild CFO from PBT using the cash-flow
statement's adjustments. Note every line where the company's presentation differs from Kaveri's (interest
classification, exceptional items, other operating income).

**E02.33** For the same company, roll forward PP&E (gross block, accumulated depreciation), leases and other equity
from the notes, and reconcile to the balance sheet. Record any gap and its explanation (OCI? disposals? FX?).

**E02.34** Find the company's tax reconciliation note and explain every reconciling item between the statutory
rate and the effective rate.
