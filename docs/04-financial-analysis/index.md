# Module 04 · Financial Statement Analysis

**What this module does.** Converts statements into judgement: growth (and what it costs), margins and cost
structure, returns on capital, working capital and cash conversion, leverage and liquidity, per-share metrics
and a full ratio dashboard, and the quality of earnings. Every lesson works Kaveri Pumps FY21–FY26 and ends with
the conclusion the numbers support.

**Why it matters.** This is the analytical core of fundamental investing: the ratios that distinguish a business
that compounds from one that merely grows, and profit that is real from profit that is reported.
