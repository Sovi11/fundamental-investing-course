## Part B (6 marks each)

**B1.** Accrual revenue = 520 + 30 (uncollected credit sales) − 15 (advance, unearned) = **₹535 Cr** (2 marks). Accrual
expenses = 410 − 12 (prepaid rent) + 8 (unpaid salaries) + 25 (depreciation) = **₹431 Cr** (2). PBT = **₹104 Cr** vs a
cash surplus of ₹110 Cr (1). The difference: revenue earned but not collected, cash received but not earned, expenses
paid in advance or not yet paid, and depreciation (non-cash) (1).

**B2.** (a) Straight-line: (200 − 20) ÷ 5 = **₹36 Cr a year**; book value after three years = 200 − 108 = **₹92 Cr** (2).
(b) Tax WDV at 40%: year 1 **₹80 Cr**, year 2 **₹48 Cr**, year 3 **₹28.8 Cr** (2). (c) End of year 1: tax base 120 vs book
164 → temporary difference 44 × 25% = **₹11 Cr DTL**. End of year 2: tax base 72 vs book 128 → 56 × 25% = **₹14 Cr DTL**
(2).

**B3.** (a) PV of ₹100 Cr for five years at 10% = **₹379.08 Cr**; ROU asset the same (1.5). (b) Depreciation 379.08 ÷ 5 =
**₹75.82 Cr**; interest 10% × 379.08 = **₹37.91 Cr**; total **₹113.72 Cr** (1.5). (c) EBITDA **+₹100 Cr** (rent removed);
EBIT **+₹24.18 Cr** (100 − 75.82); PBT **−₹13.72 Cr** (113.72 vs 100) — front-loaded cost in early years (2). (d) Closing
liability = 379.08 + 37.91 − 100 = **₹316.99 Cr** (1).

**B4.** (a) Receivable days 346.7 ÷ 1,318 × 365 = **96**; inventory days 188.1 ÷ 858 × 365 = **80**; payable days 143.4 ÷
858 × 365 = **61**; cash conversion cycle 96 + 80 − 61 = **115 days** (4). (b) Receivables at 70 days = 1,318 × 70 ÷ 365 =
₹252.8 Cr → **₹93.9 Cr released**, more than Kaveri's entire FY26 CFO of ₹65.1 Cr (2). See
[Kaveri running example](../../appendix/running-example/kaveri-pumps.md).

**B5.** Basic EPS = 120 ÷ 10 = **₹12.00** (1). Options (treasury-stock method): 1 − 1 × 150 ÷ 300 = 0.5 Cr incremental
shares, no earnings adjustment → EPS 120 ÷ 10.5 = ₹11.43 (dilutive) (2). Convertible: interest saved after tax = 200 × 8%
× 0.75 = ₹12 Cr for 0.8 Cr shares → incremental EPS ₹15.00, above ₹11.43 → **anti-dilutive; exclude** (2). Diluted EPS =
**₹11.43** (1). (Including the bond would give ₹11.68 — the classic error.)
