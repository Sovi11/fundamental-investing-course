# Module 11 · Exercises

## Warm-up

**E11.01** Write the funnel's stages with their time budgets and gate questions.

**E11.02** List six idea sources and the kind of mispricing each targets.

**E11.03** Define: variant perception; thesis-breaker; pre-mortem; say-do ratio; circle of competence.

**E11.04** State the Kelly formula for a binary bet and the continuous approximation; state the max-loss rule.

**E11.05** List the four legitimate reasons to sell and three illegitimate ones.

**E11.06** State the current STCG and LTCG rates on listed equity, the exemption threshold, and how F&O income is
taxed.

**E11.07** Name six biases and the process antidote for each.

## Core

**E11.08** Write a Screener query for "consistent compounders" (10-year sales and profit growth, average ROCE,
low leverage) and one for "improving cheap stocks" (P/B, Piotroski proxies); list two traps for each.

**E11.09** Run a 30-minute kill test on Kaveri using only the running-example pages; write the one-page sheet and
the verdict.

**E11.10** Write Kaveri's triage note in eight bullets: unit economics, industry position, say-do, first reverse
DCF, variant perception, refutation evidence.

**E11.11** Rewrite the Kaveri memo's header, variant perception and three thesis bullets from the *bull* side, as
strongly as the evidence allows.

**E11.12** Write four thesis-breakers for a long position in Nirmal Finance at ₹402, each observable and dated.

**E11.13** Compute Kelly (numerically) for the reference scenario tree from ₹260, then with a 12% chance of −80%,
and the max-loss size at a 2.5% budget; apply the "lower of" rule and the Amber overlay.

**E11.14** A ₹1.5 Cr portfolio wants 5% in a stock with ₹2 Cr delivery ADV. Compute the position as a multiple of
ADV and days to exit at 20% participation, in normal and halved-liquidity conditions.

**E11.15** Ten positions of 35% vol with pairwise correlation 0.5: compute portfolio vol; then with 25 positions;
then with 10 positions at correlation 0.8. What does the third case represent?

**E11.16** Design the quarterly review for Kaveri after Q2 FY27 with these results: revenue +9%, EBITDA margin
13.2%, H1 receivable days 101, pledge unchanged, ECL raised to ₹9 Cr. Fill each section and decide the action for
a 4% position bought at ₹260.

**E11.17** Compute tax on: LTCG ₹6 lakh and STCG ₹1.5 lakh in one year; then with a ₹2 lakh short-term loss
harvested.

**E11.18** A holding is up 90% in 10 months; the bull case is 8% above the price; it is 11% of the portfolio. Apply
the re-underwriting test and the tax consideration; decide.

**E11.19** Your journal shows 12 decisions at 75% confidence, of which 6 came true. Compute the calibration gap and
prescribe two changes.

**E11.20** For a stock with an ATM straddle at 7% of spot into results, an expected in-line print, and historical
±3% reactions, design (a) the trade if long, (b) if flat, and state the Indian F&O constraint that could stop you.

**E11.21** You are long a 1.4-beta small cap at 5% and short Nifty futures against it. Compute the hedge notional
and list what the hedge does not remove.

**E11.22** Compare a cash-secured put at ₹260 with a limit order at ₹260 for Kaveri (if it were F&O-eligible):
what does the put pay you, and what does it commit you to that the limit order does not?

## Stretch

**E11.23** Build a portfolio pre-mortem for a ten-name Indian small-cap book ("2028: −40% vs Nifty −15% — how?")
and name the shared thesis variables you would cap.

**E11.24** Write the decision-journal entry for a hypothetical purchase of Kaveri at ₹255 in March 2027 after
receivable days print at 82.

**E11.25** Argue whether a fundamental investor should ever use full Kelly; give the strongest case for each
side.

## Real-world task

**E11.26** Run a screen and an event scan this week; queue ten ideas with sources; kill-test five; triage one;
record everything in the casebook queue and kill log.

**E11.27** Write a one-page memo on a real company you have researched through Modules 03–09, size it with the
rules, and file the decision-journal entry (even if the decision is "watch").

**E11.28** Compute your own year's capital-gains tax position from your broker's statement and identify any
harvesting opportunities before 31 March.
