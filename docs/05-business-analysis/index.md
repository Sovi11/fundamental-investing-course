# Module 05 · Business & Competitive Analysis

**What this module does.** Moves from the numbers to what produces them: how a business makes money and its
unit economics; the industry structure that sets the ceiling on returns; moats and how to test for them in the
numbers; the capital cycle; management and capital allocation; corporate governance in India's promoter-led
market; and scuttlebutt and primary research within the law.

**Why it matters.** Valuation's biggest inputs — growth duration and the return on new capital — are judgements
about competition and people. This module is where those judgements are made with evidence rather than
adjectives.
