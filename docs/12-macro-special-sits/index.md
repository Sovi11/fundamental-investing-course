# Module 12 · Macro, Cycles & Special Situations

**What this module does.** Gives a bottom-up investor the macro they need — rates and equity duration,
inflation, the rupee, RBI transmission, fiscal capex, crude, the earnings cycle, and a sector × macro matrix to
audit a portfolio's exposures; market cycles and sentiment measured rather than predicted (valuation bands, flows,
IPO cycles, India's small-cap cycles, the pendulum); and special situations — demergers, buybacks, open offers,
delisting, mergers, rights, IPOs, index changes, holdco events — where structure creates the mispricing.

**Why it matters.** Every valuation embeds macro inputs and every portfolio carries factor bets the investor
did not consciously choose; and the 2025–26 demerger wave is the richest special-situations field India has had.
